# Bereavement leave

Brightwell Labs gives every employee paid bereavement leave when someone close to them dies. You do not need to use paid time off for it.

# How many days

You get up to 10 working days of paid bereavement leave for the death of a partner, child or parent, and up to 5 working days for a sibling, grandparent or other close family member. The days do not have to be taken in one block and can be used within three months.

# How to request it

Tell your manager as soon as you can and record the leave in the HR portal. You do not need to provide a death certificate.

# Extra support

If you need more time, talk to your manager or the People Team about unpaid leave or a phased return. The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling.
